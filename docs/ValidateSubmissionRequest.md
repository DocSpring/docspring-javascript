# DocSpring.ValidateSubmissionRequest

## Properties

Name | Type | Description | Notes
------------ | ------------- | ------------- | -------------
**test** | **Boolean** | Test PDFs have a watermark and are free. Live PDFs use credits. Submissions made with a test API token are always test submissions, and the demo API token's are always live. | [optional] 
**editable** | **Boolean** | `true` keeps the PDF's text and check fields fillable, and `false` flattens every field. Left out, the template decides: its `editable_submissions` setting, or each field's own Editable option. A response shows the template's setting when the request left this out. Can't be `true` with `signature_requests`. | [optional] 
**data** | **{String: Object}** | The values of the template's fields, by field name. Required for PDF templates, Word templates and page designs, unless `signature_requests` collects them (PDF templates only). | [optional] 
**metadata** | **{String: Object}** | Your own data about the submission, such as an ID from your app. It isn't printed on the PDF. Two keys also change the PDF: `pdf_filename` names its file (`files.pdf.filename`, and its download), and `pdf_title` is its title in the PDF's document properties (and an HTML template's `&lt;title&gt;`, unless its HTML has one). Without `pdf_title`, the title is the template's name. In `pdf_filename`, a `.pdf` ending is removed, each run of characters other than letters, digits, `-`, `_` and `.` becomes one `_`, and the first 128 characters are kept, then `.pdf` is added. Without it, the file is named after the submission's ID (`sub_1234567890abcdef01.pdf`). | [optional] 
**password** | **String** | A password that the PDF needs to be opened with. It's never returned; `has_password` says if it's set. Word documents and OpenDocument files can't be password-protected, so a submission with a password can't make them. | [optional] 
**output_formats** | [**[OutputFormat]**](OutputFormat.md) | What the submission makes: `pdf`, `docx` (a Word document, from a Word template), `odt` (an OpenDocument file, the format LibreOffice uses, converted from a Word template's Word document), and `png` (an image of each page of the PDF, up to 100 pages, which adds `pdf`) when you ask for it. Default `["pdf"]`, or a Word template's `output_format`. POST /submissions/{submission_id}/generate_files adds `png`, and a Word template's submission's `pdf`, `docx` or `odt`, to a processed submission. | [optional] 
**expires_in** | **Number** | Seconds after the PDF is generated until the submission expires, up to 10 years (315360000). Default the template's expiration setting. A submission that isn't processed, such as one with invalid data, is kept for at least 48 hours. The demo API token's submissions expire within an hour: up to 3600, and that's their default. | [optional] 
**field_overrides** | [**{String: FieldOverride}**](FieldOverride.md) | Changes to field settings for this submission only, by field name, such as `{"name": {"required": false, "font_size": 9}}`. A setting that identifies the field (`id`, `name`, `type`), that only the template sets (such as `description`), or that doesn't apply to the field's type and display type (such as `check_character` on a field displayed as text) is a 422. With `signature_requests`, `editable: true` is a 422, and a `default` for a signature or initials field makes the submission `invalid_data`. | [optional] 
**html** | **String** | For an HTML template, HTML to use instead of the template's. | [optional] 
**css** | **String** | For an HTML template, SCSS (plain CSS works too) added after the template's styles, on the page and in its header and footer. It doesn't replace them: its rules come later, so they win over the template's rules of the same specificity. | [optional] 
**signature_requests** | [**[SignatureRequestInput]**](SignatureRequestInput.md) | The people who sign the PDF, and fill in their fields first. The PDF is generated once every signature request is completed. PDF templates only. Without `role`s, the first signature request is `Signer 1`, the second `Signer 2`, and so on. To leave a signer out, give every signature request its `role` and leave that one's out. Only the signers sign: a value for a signature or initials field in `data` makes the submission `invalid_data`. | [optional] 
**send_email** | **Boolean** | Whether DocSpring emails the signers their signing links, and sends the reminders and the completed document. Default true. Each signature request's own `send_email` overrides it. A test submission only emails your DocSpring team: every other address is a 422. `true` needs `signature_requests`. DocSpring doesn't email the signers of a form library template: leave `send_email` out, and send them their links yourself. | [optional] 
**signing_order** | [**SigningOrder**](SigningOrder.md) | `parallel` (the default): everyone can sign at once. `sequential`: people sign one step at a time (`signing_step`), and DocSpring emails each step when the one before it is done. Needs `signature_requests`. | [optional] 
**email_subject** | **String** | The subject of the emails that ask people to sign. You can use `{{ signer.name }}`, `{{ signer.email }}`, `{{ document.name }}`, `{{ sender.name }}` and `{{ account.name }}`. Default "&lt;sender&gt; sent you &lt;document&gt; to sign" when you leave it out (a blank subject is a 422). Links and web addresses are shown as plain text (`example[.]com`). | [optional] 
**email_message** | **String** | A message for the people who sign, shown above the signing button. It takes the same variables as `email_subject`. Links and web addresses are shown as plain text. Leave it out for none: a blank message is a 422. | [optional] 
**cc** | **[String]** | People who get a copy of the completed document. They don't sign. | [optional] 
**reply_to** | **String** | Where replies to the emails go: a member of your DocSpring team, or an address at one of your verified email domains. Leave it out for the template's reply-to address (the account owner when it has none). | [optional] 
**notification_emails** | **[String]** | Who DocSpring tells when the document is completed or declined, or an email bounces or can't be sent: members of your DocSpring team or addresses at your verified email domains. Leave it out for the template's notification addresses (the account owner when it has none); an empty list means the account owner. | [optional] 
**notify_when_signed** | **Boolean** | Also email `notification_emails` each time someone signs. Default false. | [optional] 
**reminders** | [**SignatureRequestReminders**](SignatureRequestReminders.md) | Remind people who haven't signed: every `interval_days` days, up to `max_count` times. Each reminder has a new signing link. Without it, DocSpring sends no reminders. | [optional]
